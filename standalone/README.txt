REACH, a Jason Production
=========================

This folder is the standalone version of REACH. It runs in any modern
browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox) with an internet connection.
It is graphics-heavy. If it runs slowly, open Settings (top right of the
main menu, or Esc > Settings during a game) and lower Graphics or Render
distance. Headphones make it much scarier.

PLAY SOLO
  Double-click index.html.

PLAY CO-OP WITH VOICE CHAT (2 to 4 players)
  Co-op and voice need the game to be online, so put it on a website:

  Option A: itch.io
    1. Make a free account at itch.io and click "Upload new project".
    2. Set "Kind of project" to HTML.
    3. Upload REACH-standalone.zip and tick "This file will be played in the browser".
    4. Set the viewport to 1280 x 720 and turn on the fullscreen button.
    5. Save. You can make it free, pay-what-you-want, or paid.

  Option B: GitHub Pages
    1. Make a public repository and upload index.html.
    2. Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch > main > Save.
    3. Your game is live at https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/REPOSITORY-NAME/

UPDATING A COPY YOU ALREADY PUT ON GITHUB PAGES
  1. Open your repository on github.com.
  2. On its front page, click "Add file" > "Upload files".
  3. Drag the new index.html in. It replaces the old one (same name).
  4. Click "Commit changes".
  5. Wait a minute or two, then reload your game page with Ctrl+F5
     (Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac) so the browser doesn't keep the old version.

LEVELS
  Level 1, Reach - B4: ten areas of a dead facility, and something tall
  that hunts by sound.
  Level 2, Seize - B5, and Level 3, Breach - A1: early access. Have the
  code? Click either card in the menu and enter it to play them now. Only
  the host needs the code: everyone in their room goes down with them.

IN THE GAME
  One player presses "Host co-op", names the room, and sends the 5-letter
  room code (or the invite link) to friends, who type it and press "Join".
  Rooms are Public: anyone can find them under "Find a room" and join
  from the list. Set a room to Private and only people with the code can
  join.
  Everyone who wants voice chat turns it on in Settings > Online.
  Teammates hear you when you are near them, and so does the creature -
  but not if you whisper (Whisper-safe mic).
  If a friend can't connect, everyone set Settings > Online > Connection
  to Relay. School, college and work Wi-Fi often block direct connections
  between players: use Relay there. Settings > Online > Check my
  connection tells you what your network lets through. If it still won't
  connect, try a phone hotspot.

CONTROLS
  WASD move, Shift run, C crouch (and crawl under low racks), F flashlight,
  E use (hold for some things), G whistle, 1-5 or the mouse wheel to pick
  an item, Q drop the item in your hand, M mute your mic, L camera lock
  (on: the camera follows the mouse; off: the cursor is free and you hold
  the mouse button and drag to look), Esc pause.
